# Product image licensing report

The release preserves all 400 photo files supplied by the project owner. The inventory by product ID is in image-review/product-list.csv and the visible assets are under public/products/produce. The original URLs, licence terms and permissions were not supplied. The images are mapped to listings, with packaging details recorded where readable. Exact pack variants and commercial usage rights cannot be inferred from a filename or search result.

Before public sale or redistribution, record each photographer or licensor, original source URL, the permitted usage and any attribution requirement. Replace any asset you cannot clear with your own product photography or supplier-approved images. Photo permissions are separate from software ownership.
