# FreshNest evaluation walkthrough

Use [README.md](README.md) for setup and [BUYER_HANDOFF.md](BUYER_HANDOFF.md) for the release scope.

1. Browse as a guest. Search, open a product, check a delivery PIN and add an item.
2. Open checkout and sign in using a configured provider. Confirm your basket carries over.
3. Review the quoted delivery fee and order total. Place a COD order or use a configured Razorpay test account.
4. Use an operator account to advance the order and assign an available delivery partner. For an approved store order, the store account accepts, picks and packs its part first.
5. The customer opens Order Tracking and reveals the delivery code at handover. The delivery partner enters it to finish the delivery.
6. Download the order receipt and review the customer's notifications.
